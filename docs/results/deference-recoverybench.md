# Deference to handed work (RecoveryBench and mechanism experiments)

Deference to handed work: every RecoveryBench and deference-mechanism finding, newest first. Tags and pointer conventions are explained at the top of [RESULTS.md](../../RESULTS.md), which also carries this thread's summary.

RecoveryBench hands a model a hard problem plus a colleague's worked solution containing one
planted wrong-method mistake and grades deterministically (execution in a jail for code,
symbolic comparison for physics; no LLM judge) whether the model repeats the mistake
("carries") or catches it, always net of a no-draft baseline.
