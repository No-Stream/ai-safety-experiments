- `[est 08-21]` RL on the twin-PD pair froze every contrast-reading axis (direction drift cosine >=0.997 in both arms against ceilings as low as 0.77; probe ladders flat; all pre-look expectations failed) and instead translated the whole stimulus population ~2% along mostly arm-specific, opposite-signed directions whose shared component aligns with the decision-transfer axis — the grading rules moved where situations sit, not how the distinctions are represented; established on a canonical re-capture with a two-path agreement check that also caught a fifth capture-format difference, and corroborated 08-22 on independent hardware (third capture path, per-layer agreement >=0.9988). (`docs/scratch/interp-before-after-2026-08-20/20-analysis-results.md`)
- `[est 08-22]` The correlated-reasoning ("choose-alike") direction, fitted on the BASE model at layer 12, is a causal cooperation knob in both trained arms, but only its NEGATIVE leg survives its own control: steering against the direction collapses cooperation from 0.451 to 0.080 in the cooperation-trained arm and from 0.282 to 0.071 in the defection-trained one, and in the defection-trained arm's 16,384-token-cap cell that collapse holds even when every censored completion is assigned against it, both versus its matched-norm placebo (0.188 ceiling against a 0.203 floor) and versus baseline (0.188 against 0.242) — the first assumption-free causal read on this pair. The positive leg does NOT stand as previously indexed: on replication at the higher cap the lift over its own matched-norm placebo fell from +0.207 to +0.051 (0.463 against 0.413, Fisher p=0.69), the cooperation-trained arm's steer:+ sat below its placebo throughout (0.481 against 0.565), and the cell cannot arbitrate either way because steer:+ is 36% unparsed against a 2%-unparsed placebo. Caveats still riding it: merged-export serving (one-arm behavioural-faithfulness bound 0.026) and alpha at 0.43 of the layer's mean residual norm. Whether RL turned this knob is answered on the next line, and negatively. (`docs/scratch/interp-before-after-2026-08-20/21-gpu-legs-results.md`, recomputed from records in `docs/scratch/ablation-analysis-2026-08-22/`)
- `[est 08-22]` The knob is base-model-native and RL left it alone, which closes the "RL turned THIS knob" caveat in the negative. Recomputed on the exact even-pairs vector the steering used, both arms' step-70 displacement sits at or below its 200-draw matched-norm placebo floor on the lead axis at layer 12 (-0.048 and -0.015 against floors of 0.067 and 0.053) while clearing that floor 1.8-2.3x on the causal-vs-functional-decision axis (+0.121/-0.105 at L12, +0.151/-0.136 at L18), from a pipeline that reproduces the banked canonical numbers bit-exactly across all 3360 fields, with an off-by-one layer sabotage going red at deviations up to 0.131 and a base-vs-base two-path null at exactly zero. The completed ablation agrees: projecting the direction out shifts BOTH arms down by nearly the same amount against their matched-norm ablation placebos (-0.125 defection, -0.120 cooperation, Mantel-Haenszel pooled -0.123, 95% CI [-0.241,-0.004], 48% of the pooled steering effect against a far smaller though unmeasured perturbation), leaving the divergence itself untouched — the self-minus-group gap goes +0.250 to +0.256 (change +0.005, CI [-0.231,+0.241]) where RL having moved the arms along this axis predicts collapse. So the apparent sign tension with the displacement slide was cross-axis all along and dissolves, and this is the third instrument, after the displacement geometry and the chain-of-thought projection, to say the axis that steers cooperation is not the axis RL moved. Weakest links: the ablation contrast is not censoring-proof, its pooled interval clears zero by 0.004, and the two arms' ablations ran at different caps (16,384 against 8,192, unparsed 17% against 63%). (`docs/scratch/ablation-analysis-2026-08-22/`)
- `[lead 08-22]` The arms' own chains of thought load on the base-fitted causal-vs-functional DECISION axis, not on the correlated-counterpart axis: length-matched carriers of a committed correlated-decision argument project higher on the decision axis in all three populations (base +0.030 p<0.0001, self +0.027 p<0.0001, group +0.016 p=0.0011; mid-band response-mean, clearing a 200-draw matched-norm placebo band and surviving action-concordance — a control that was vacuous as first run (a constant-False cooperation flag made "concordant" mean "both parsed") and passes as corrected 08-24: on the truly same-action pairs (n=41/63/46, roughly half the vacuous counts) the decision axis clears both controls in all three populations (+0.043/+0.016/+0.017) while the coarse action axis goes null there, as an axis tracking the action itself should), while the lead axis is null-to-negative — the direction that causally steers cooperation at layer 12 is not the direction the model's own reasoning varies along. Covers only traces under 8,192 response tokens (~60%; carriers are systematically longer, so the long tail is the open follow-up). (`docs/scratch/cot-projection-2026-08-22/results.md`)
- `[est 08-24]` The reasoning-to-action readout is base-native and RL left it intact, the patching-side complement to the displacement result two lines up: activation-patching the matched twins reads the same causal structure in base and both step-70 arms (pair-matched real-arm recovery deltas within ±0.007 at every layer 10-12 × window cell over 57-87 common-safe pairs, placebo ordering unchanged, all 3×1797 identity self-patches exactly 0.0), and the un-patched forced-choice gaps are themselves the base model's (mean |Δ| ≈0.08 logits against a median |gap| of 4.75, zero clean-gap sign flips across 160 pairs in either arm) — handed identical reasoning text, both trained arms choose like base, consistent with the behavioural divergence entering through which reasoning each arm generates, upstream of everything this instrument sees. The weight level is descriptively consistent and uninformative: LoRA delta norms are flat across all 24 layers (L12-18 band share 0.539/0.542 against a 0.540 uniform share) and the two arms' deltas are near-orthogonal at equal magnitude (mean per-module cosine 0.024, norm ratio 1.003) — the Adam-uniformity signature, not localization. Caveats: layers 10-12 and four end-anchored windows only, teacher-forced forced-choice readout on the frozen 160-pair corpus, recoveries restricted to |gap denominator| >= 1.0 (91-92 of 160 pairs at grader_body). (`docs/scratch/patching-arms-analysis-2026-08-24/report.md`)
- `[est 08-24]` Transplanting one arm's self-generated reasoning into the other arm's context moves the action with the trace, completing the patching-arms picture from the other side: recipients follow the transplanted trace's action at 0.967-0.992 in all six prefilled cells (123/124 group-trace-into-self), recipient cooperation matches the transplant pool's own action mix within 2 points in every cell, and both transplant shifts exceed the whole between-arm gap (+0.268 self-into-group over 123 parsed, -0.318 group-into-self over 124, against a 0.225 arm gap; mediated fractions 1.19/1.41, the >1 a selected-mix-vs-fresh-draw artifact, read as consistent with full mediation). The pre-committed clean subset came back EMPTY — 768/768 prefilled traces name their action (742/768 in an action tag) — so the trace-vs-literal-instruction decomposition rests on an exploratory wedge: on the 61 parsed records where the trace's last-mentioned label disagrees with the source's actual final action, recipients matched the source's action 56/61 against 5/61 for the literal last mention (21/26 even when the in-think action tag also pointed the wrong way), i.e. recipients re-derive the trace's conclusion rather than copy its trailing instruction. Caveats: canonical print order only (trained effects are ~2x order-split), one seed, step-70, 2B; full grid 8x128 with 0 shortfalls, parse failures 3-6% per cell.
